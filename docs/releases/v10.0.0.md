# NOOP v10.0.0

A release about making NOOP feel like yours, without changing what it is. You can now colour the app
with a chosen accent, save a whole look as a preset, and put your own photo behind every screen. The
workout picker learned dozens of new sports, a run can carry its distance and a GPS route you can
export, and the Workouts screen grew a heatmap of your recent effort. Alongside the personalisation,
a batch of sleep and calorie work makes several numbers more honest. Everything still runs on your own
device, offline, with no account.

## Scores that change

Read this before wondering why a number moved. A few changes alter values you have already seen.

- **Active calories use a fitness-adjusted model when your resting heart rate is known (#1268).** NOOP
  now estimates burn with the Keytel VO₂max-based equation instead of the age/weight-only one, which
  moves already-stored calorie figures. History computed before this stays as it was; new days use the
  better model.
- **Oura sleep reads less phantom wake (#1246, #1248, #1293, #1296).** Erased and partly-written
  hypnogram pages were being decoded as hours of "awake"; they are now correctly treated as a gap, so
  affected Oura nights show higher efficiency and less wake. A night scored before the fix keeps its
  old value until re-imported.
- **An Oura night is scored from the ring's own staging (#1183).** Feeding Oura's hypnogram straight
  into the scorer, rather than re-deriving it, changes the stage split on Oura nights.
- **Sleep-stage percentages sum to exactly 100 (#1282),** and the nap card's "Main sleep" now describes
  the whole bridged night to match the hero and iPhone (#1266, #1267) — small display shifts, not new
  measurements.
- **Android sleep Consistency matches the iPhone definition (#1233).** Both platforms now compute it
  from bedtime-onset spread, so the two stop disagreeing.
- **Stress is steadier (#1114, #1116).** The daytime timeline masks exertion instead of scoring
  movement as stress, and an HRV dip now has to sustain before it nudges the score.

## New

**Make it yours.** A chrome accent colour you choose — Mint, WHOOP Blue, or a custom colour from a full
HSV picker (#1171, #1177) — with Android brought to brand-mint parity with iPhone (#1170). Named theme
presets coordinate the accent, charts, backdrop and cards as one look (#1172). And a custom background
image sits behind every tab, on all three platforms (#1234, #1235, #1236), with the Liquid hero card
made theme-aware in light mode along the way (#1160, #1162).

**Workouts.** Around forty new sports across two passes (#1273, #1274), plus a Start-and-Add action row
and sport-icon picker on Android (#1079). A manual workout takes a Distance field (#1203); a GPS workout
records live distance and pace (#1202) and writes manual and edited workouts back to Health Connect
(#1204); and a finished route exports to GPX or FIT (#1238, #1239). The Workouts screen gains a 13-week
active-calorie heatmap (#1240), and Compare charts gain value scrubbing (#529).

**Sleep.** Choose the hypnogram's look — Classic, Fill, Garmin Fill or Ribbon — each with a colour-coded
stage legend, on every platform (#1129, #1283, #1290, #1291). Today can now host your Trends and Sleep
cards — Sleep marks, Asleep duration, and more — so your most-watched detail lives on the home screen
(#1230, #1231, #1232), and a "sleep may be incomplete" caveat appears on sparse-motion nights (#1096).

**Metrics and language.** A language setting independent of the phone (#1181, #1004). Skin temperature
can read as an absolute °C or a Δ from your baseline (#1224). WHOOP 5.0 SpO₂ is available behind an
experimental toggle (#1185), and the WHOOP 4.0 battery-pack voltage path is decoded (#1279). Per-event
opt-in haptics for in-session strap cues, and an opt-in wrist buzz for the phone's own timer and alarm
(#1115, #1158, #1159).

## Fixed

- **A connected strap with nothing arriving no longer flickers the daily history to empty (#1196),** and
  a post-offload re-score stops erasing days it should have kept.
- **A WHOOP 5.0 mislabelled as a 4.0 is corrected from the strap's own DIS attestation (#1175),** and a
  5.0/MG can turn Broadcast-HR off again with an honest log line (#1061).
- **Two straps no longer lose a row to a duplicated day (#1190),** and the charging pill flips on the
  actual charge event rather than only on a battery-level change (#1189).
- **The strap log keeps its last three generations (#1259, #1263),** so a restart stops erasing the very
  cause it was recording.
- **A non-WHOOP device stops inheriting WHOOP-5 empty-state copy (#1285),** respiratory rate is correct
  for BLE-only WHOOP users (#1178), and the Android SpO₂ and Rest-ring captions are localised and sized
  (#1184, #1186).
- **Oura reconnects survive CoreBluetooth suspension (#1286),** 0x5D battery buckets are read oldest-first
  (#1182), and banked-sleep duplicates heal across every device rather than one id (#1248).

## Under the hood

Analytics and the live-HR hot path shed avoidable churn with no change to any value (#1214). A pure
primary-session resting-HR definition is recorded as a shadow metric with its coverage inputs, ahead of
any wiring (#1169, #1174, #1188, #1211). Forced re-scores are attributed as new-data yes/no so a battery
investigation can tell a real refresh from a spin (#1192), and a raft of Oura protocol diagnostics make
the decode legible from the strap log alone (#1284 and the #1043–#1131 series).
