# NOOP v11.8.0

A release about doing more on the strap itself, and about turning off the parts you do not want.

## A gym log book, advanced from your wrist

NOOP now carries an on-device lift log on iPhone and Mac. Build a session, and move between sets with a
double-tap on the strap rather than picking the phone up mid-set.

Android gets the groundwork rather than the book: the new schema with its matching Room twin, and the
same set-metrics engine, so the numbers are computed by one implementation on both platforms rather than
by two that can drift apart. The log book itself is not on Android yet, tracked in #2327.

## A Coach that switches off

The AI Coach now has a single master switch. Turning it off retires the tab, the generated brief, the
home-screen widget and the tray notification, and it stops every request that would otherwise leave the
device. This is the whole feature going quiet, not just the screen being hidden.

Its settings moved onto their own screen rather than sitting in a crowded list. Android gained the
bottom-bar tab that iPhone already had, Disconnect is now somewhere an iPhone can reach, the current
OpenAI models are offered with the catalogue kept up to date, and a rate-limited request shows what the
provider actually said instead of a generic failure.

## Sync the strap from a shortcut

A Sync Strap App Intent runs a sync from Shortcuts, the Lock Screen or a Home Screen button. A Live
Activity shows the offload while it runs, and an optional setting keeps the screen awake for as long as
it takes.

Reconnecting when the app is off-screen changed underneath that. iOS throttles background scanning hard
enough that a strap could sit undiscovered for minutes and then appear seconds after the app came to the
foreground. NOOP now asks for the last strap by name in the background, which iOS does honour, and keeps
scanning in the foreground so a strap you have switched to is still found.

## Workouts that are easier to keep tidy

Sessions shorter than a minute are discarded rather than cluttering the list, and the list itself splits
into Current and Archived so a long history stays navigable. Deleting a workout now clears it from every
namespace the list reads, including the copy Apple Health had kept after the store let go of it.

The WHOOP sports that were missing are selectable, and the icons those exposed are fixed.
Auto-detection no longer saves anything on its own: it asks, and waits.

## Readings that admit when they failed

A failed WHOOP 5 flag read was being reported as though it were a value. It now says it failed, on both
platforms. The frame integrity verdict reaches every consumer intact rather than being narrowed on the
way through, and the strap family NOOP records is the one the connection actually established rather
than the one it initially picked.

On Oura, a notification that tiles exactly into several packets is now read to the end instead of
stopping at the first. A young ring no longer adopts an anchor unit ten times too large, settled by
adjacency rather than assumption, and a diagnostic reports which ring epoch each sidecar row believes
in.

## Charts that stop redrawing everything

Workouts and Sleep were re-evaluating their entire screens on every live heart-rate tick, roughly once a
second whenever a strap was connected. They no longer do. Three charts that had no hover gained a
tooltip, two uncapped series are downsampled before drawing, the sleep motion trace computes its peak
once rather than per epoch, and the Apple stress level rounds the way Android's already did.

The steps charts are clearer, with an optional 30-day average card.

## Apple Health that keeps up

A re-score that was deferred now publishes to Apple Health once it lands, rather than being missed
because the publish already ran. Workouts are observed for live delivery, and a pass running in the
background paces itself under the CPU limit iOS enforces instead of being killed by it. A CSV import no
longer turns a correct SDNN into RMSSD on the way back out.

## Localization

Chinese is complete in both scripts, across the app and the watch complications. The French recovery and
strain words are repaired, and the theme picker says light rather than lightweight.

A gap that had been letting UI copy ship English in every locale is closed. The scan that checks whether
a string has a catalogue entry was not looking at the spelling the codebase tells you to prefer, so
nothing reported strings that had none. Closing it surfaced the Home screen's own labels and the terms
you agree to on first launch, both of which are now translated.
